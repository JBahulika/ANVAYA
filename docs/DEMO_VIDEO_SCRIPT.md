# Demo video script (60–90 seconds)

Record screen + voiceover on a phone or laptop. Prefer **Chrome**, **HTTPS** deploy (or localhost for recording). Show the UI, not slides.

## Shot list

| Time | Visual | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Read screen + a bill on the table | “ANVAYA is a document reader for people who cannot see the page.” |
| 0:08–0:15 | Tap **Talk** or **Capture & hear** | “Point at a bill. One tap. Hear what you owe.” |
| 0:15–0:40 | Utility bill in frame → answer spoken | “Amount due and due date — first. Not a dump of every line.” |
| 0:40–0:55 | Same photo → Simplify | “First, then, finally — what to do next.” |
| 0:55–1:10 | Optional Ask: amount due | “Ask any field on the page.” |
| 1:10–1:25 | Trust line | “Photos are not stored. Not financial advice. Key stays on the server.” |
| 1:25–1:30 | End card | “Live demo. Link in the description.” |

## B-roll tips

- Good lighting on the bill.
- Do **not** film crossing a street or a subway platform as “safety.”
- If Talk fails with speech-network errors, record **Capture & hear** as the hero path and mention voice agents verbally.

## Export

- 1080p, landscape or phone portrait — match Unstop limits.
- Title: `ANVAYA — Voice-first accessibility copilot`
- Description: live demo URL + GitHub URL.
